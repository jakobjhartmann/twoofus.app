Frequently asked questions

Yes — TwoOfUs uses Sign in with Apple. It’s the only sign-in method, so there’s no extra password. Your Apple account keeps your data private and syncs it through iCloud.

One of you generates an invite code in the format XXXX-XXXX. Share it with your partner, who enters it in their app. Once the code is accepted, your devices are paired and your widgets start syncing.

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area until the icons jiggle, tap the + (or Edit → Add Widget), search for TwoOfUs, pick a size and place it. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, select a widget area and add the TwoOfUs widget you want.

The Distance widget needs location permission to work out how far apart you are. Open Settings → TwoOfUs → Location and make sure access is allowed. Both partners need location enabled for the distance to appear.

You share one note of up to 100 characters. When you write a new one, it replaces the old one in the widget and your partner gets a notification. That’s how a quick “thinking of you” lands right on their screen. Earlier notes are kept in your shared message history in the app.

The Partner clock shows your partner’s current local time with their name and city — ideal for long-distance relationships across time zones. It’s derived from their location and updates every minute, so you always know whether it’s morning or midnight over there.

The flower card is a date invitation you unwrap: you choose a date idea, write a card, tie a bouquet from flowers, paper and ribbon, and send it off. Your partner unwraps the bouquet in the app and finds your card inside. They can accept or suggest a different time; TwoOfUs converts the times into both time zones automatically. Accepted dates can be added to your iOS calendar, and the countdown starts on its own.

Your partner — and only at the right moment: if the seal is on, the app shows your answers to your partner only once you’ve both submitted yours, and vice versa. Then both open at the same time. The answers are stored and synced to both devices as soon as they are submitted, in your shared iCloud space. So the seal is a display rule in the app, not encryption. We ourselves have no access to your answers.

On a check-in day you get a reminder at your chosen time, and in the morning too if you like. If your check-in is still open in the evening, a last call follows about 90 minutes later (never after 11:30 pm). If you skipped the last check-in, the app also reminds you at midday on the next check-in day. As soon as you submit, that day’s reminders disappear. You can turn off the extra reminders in the check-in settings under “Extra reminders”.

A shared jar for the loveliest lines from your check-ins. When your partner writes something that deserves to stay, you drop it in with “Add to jar”. Later, either of you can draw a slip and read it again, any time. Skipped check-in weeks are fine — the jar keeps filling anyway.

In your private iCloud database at Apple — specifically in the iCloud of whichever of you created the invitation. The other reaches the same data through an iCloud share. Only whoever redeems your invite code gets access, so share it only with each other. We ourselves have no access to your content. There are no servers of ours and no tracking. You’ll find the details in the privacy policy.

Only if you want it to, and only to write: after an accepted date invitation you can tap “Add to iOS Calendar”, and the app creates exactly that one event. TwoOfUs never reads your calendar.

When you share an invitation as a link, the invitation data is encoded in the link fragment — the part after the #. That fragment never leaves the browser and isn’t sent to any server. Only someone who has the link can read the invitation. After the date, the invitation page no longer shows it, but the link itself still contains the data. So only share it with your partner.

“Always” keeps the Distance widget and Partner clock current even when the app is closed. “While Using” works too, but only updates while the app is open, so widget data can go a little stale. For the smoothest experience, we recommend “Always”.

In the app under Settings → Account → “Delete Account”. This signs you out and resets the app data on your device. If you created the invitation, the app also deletes your shared data in your iCloud: in version 1.7 only while you are still connected, from version 1.8 always. If you joined with a code, your access ends; from version 1.8 your profile and location record is deleted as well. You can also delete data in your own iCloud at any time in iOS: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → TwoOfUs. We have no access to your iCloud data. If you have questions, jakobsworld@web.de is happy to help; the details are in the privacy policy.

TwoOfUs requires iOS 26 or later. If your iPhone runs iOS 26 or newer, you’re good to go.
